# x360launcher (beta version)

x360launcher is an Xbox-style dashboard for Windows. It brings your PC games, Xbox 360 games, web apps, videos, music, comics and files together on one screen. You can use an Xbox controller or a keyboard, and the look and feel follow the classic Xbox experience.

## Home and sections

The home screen shows large and small tiles, like an Xbox dashboard. The top bar has sections for Home, Bing, Social, Video, Games, Music, Apps and Settings. Each section has its own tiles, and you can add, remove, move or restore them. Removed tiles can be brought back from **Settings > Restore removed tiles**.

## Games

- Launch PC games from your library or from a file path. The **Recently played** row shows the games you opened last.
- Run Xbox 360 games (ISO files and extracted games) through Xenia. Xenia is included with the app, and you can also place your own copy in the `xenia` folder.
- Game icons are read automatically from the game files.
- Achievements and your Gamerscore are shown on your profile.

## Xbox Guide

Press the Guide button on your controller, or press G or Tab, to open an Xbox 360 style guide. From there you can move between sections, open Settings, switch users or close the app. The Home button opens a quick menu for jumping to sections.

## Web and Bing

The **Bing** section opens a web window inside the app. You can search, open pages, go back and forward, reload, save favorites and view your history. Shortcut tiles for Telegram, WhatsApp, YouTube, Spotify, Gmail, GitHub and Wikipedia can be added to any section. The full-screen button hides the top bar so you can watch videos on the whole screen.

The controller pointer works like a mouse on web pages: click links and buttons with A, open hover menus, and scroll with the right stick.

## Video, music and comics

- **Video** plays your movies and videos from the folders you choose, with full-screen and playback controls.
- **Music** plays songs from your own music library.
- **Comics** opens comic book files and lets you read them page by page.

## Files

A built-in file manager lets you browse, create folders, rename, delete and add files. The app asks for permission before it accesses folders on your PC.

## Profiles and avatars

When you create a profile, you choose a gamertag. The app also suggests gamertags. Then you build a 3D avatar by choosing gender, skin, hair, eyes, clothes and animation. You can create more than one profile.

## Settings

- **Display**: choose the dashboard screen and the game screen, and what the dashboard does while a game runs (stay behind the game, move to another screen or hide).
- **Appearance**: ready-made themes, accent colors, gray or custom backgrounds, tile styles and lighting effects.
- **Animations**: animation style and speed.
- **Customize pages**: personalize the section pages.
- **Tile personalization**: change tile sizes and positions.
- **Keyboard languages**: turn on on-screen keyboard layouts such as Persian, Arabic, French, Russian, German, Spanish and Turkish. Press LANG on the on-screen keyboard to switch between the languages you turned on.
- **System**: system status, Bluetooth (list devices, turn them on or off, forget a device, open Windows pairing), Wi-Fi, full screen and reset settings.
- **Account** and **Profile**: manage your account and profile.

## Controller and keyboard

The app is designed for an Xbox controller and works with a keyboard too. The on-screen keyboard opens with the controller for typing in search boxes and when creating a profile. While it is open, the pointer is paused so it does not move under the keys. Press B to close it.

## Updates

The installed app checks for a newer version when it starts. If one is available, it downloads in the background, and you choose when to restart and install it.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit
- An Xbox controller or a keyboard
- Only games you own legally

## Notes

- Windows 7 is  added in this version.
- Use only games and media you have the right to play.
